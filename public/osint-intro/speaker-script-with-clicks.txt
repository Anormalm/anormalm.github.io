Across five tasks and twenty-two digests, I ran fifteen executions. At cap one, four out of five outputs were valid, and twenty-three of thirty-one fields matched. More searching helped in this small setup, but these numbers measure digest agreement; we haven’t evaluated live-web performance or compared against a baseline agent.
