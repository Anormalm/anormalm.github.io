Study details and downloads

The same 24 graphs were tested with original, identical-repeat, neutral and degree-reversed names at 16,384 and 32,768 completion tokens. Providers and settings were pinned. Wrong, invalid and cutoff responses were never retried.

Accuracy includes invalid and cutoff responses in its denominator. Length finishes count every provider-reported length stop, including those with a correct answer; this differs from the leaderboard’s unsuccessful-cutoff metric.

Four prompts per graph are correlated. These are small-sample development results, not a model ranking or a change to Hard v1’s 16K protocol. Shared token caps do not imply equal native compute. Full metrics include confidence intervals, repeated-input agreement, renaming survival and paired outcome transitions.
