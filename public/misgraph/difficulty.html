How to read these scores

Renaming preserves the graph and its correct answer. An identical-prompt repeat measures ordinary answer variation. Wrong answers, invalid answers and cutoffs remain in the returned-response denominator. A shared token cap does not give every model equal computation.

The paired analysis compares renamed accuracy with identical-repeat accuracy. None of the 18 planned contrasts passes Holm correction. Confidence intervals and all denominators are in the analysis JSON. There are four independent graphs per task and size; the four conditions are not independent samples.

Protocol, coverage and downloads

Six tasks: weighted shortest path, shortest-path count, exact-walk count, triangle count, minimum cut and constrained reachability. Each uses four graphs at each of three sizes. Providers and settings were fixed per configuration. Collection stopped when no eligible work remained; there are no outstanding reservations.

GLM was blocked by provider failures; Gemini stopped after a protocol-invalid response. Granite and Qwen each exhausted the permitted infrastructure recovery on one slot. Those gaps limit comparisons.

All 72 exact-answer certificates and 288 prompt checks passed. The study is exploratory development work; these scores do not replace the frozen leaderboard.
