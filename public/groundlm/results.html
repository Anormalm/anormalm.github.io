Read the pattern

Two failure profiles.

A small semantic gap can coexist with broad instability. Compare the full set of diagnostics, not just one score.

A

Targeted semantic interference

DeepSeek's consistency falls from 96.9% to 70.1% under semantic names while format, order and distractor stability remain high. gpt-5.4-mini retains 53.3% of originally correct answers.

B

Broad instability

Qwen and Llama degrade across diagnostics. Their smaller semantic gaps (6.1 and 9.1 points) reflect already-low consistency under neutral names.

Metric field guide

Know what each number means.

Accuracy

Share of baseline answers matching the graph-derived ground truth.

1 − FS

Format instability: disagreement across the six pairs of four graph formats.

OS

Order sensitivity: prediction changes under reordered serializations.

Δsem = ICN − ICM

Neutral minus semantic-name consistency. A larger positive gap indicates more semantic interference.

1 − DR

Susceptibility to irrelevant, answer-preserving graph additions.

CFM

Among originally correct answers, the share that stays correct after semantic renaming.
